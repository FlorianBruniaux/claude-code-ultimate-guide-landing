What are the five current DORA software delivery metrics?

---

The current DORA model has five software delivery metrics. Throughput uses Change Lead Time, Deployment Frequency, and Failed Deployment Recovery Time. Instability uses Change Fail Rate and Deployment Rework Rate. DORA narrowed the historical MTTR metric to recovery from a failed deployment in 2023 and added Deployment Rework Rate in 2024.
